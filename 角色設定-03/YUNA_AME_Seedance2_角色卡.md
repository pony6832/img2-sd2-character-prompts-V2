## Test Checklist

- First test should be 4-6 seconds, vertical 9:16, medium shot, slow movement only.
- Identity must stay stable: high loose bun, loose face strands, delicate oval face, blush-pink top, translucent cardigan, pearl bracelet, small pendant.
- If face drifts, use closer framing and reduce head movement.
- If hands break, avoid complicated V-sign transitions; use bracelet touch or cardigan hold.
- If styling becomes too revealing, repeat: `tasteful casual styling, modest blush-pink ribbed knit top, translucent cardigan, cinematic lifestyle mood`.
